Selected Accounts/Influencers


Influencer accounts were selected through a role-based purposive sampling strategy.
An account was eligible if it held at least one of the following roles:
(i) official project or foundation account,
(ii) founder or core contributor,
(iii) major corporate or public advocate with documented asset-related activity,
(iv) sovereign or policy actor whose decisions directly concern the asset, or
(v) token namesake or central figure of the token brand.
These roles are binary and publicly documentable, which makes the selection reproducible
and avoids arbitrary cutoffs inherent to popularity-based rankings.

This approach was chosen to pre-select accounts whose posts are plausibly relevant
as public information events for the corresponding cryptocurrency.
In this report, we analyze the tweets of these influencers in combination with minute-level market price data.


Selected Accounts/Influencers:

BTC

- Jack Dorsey(iii): selected because of his public Bitcoin advocacy and his role in Twitter/X and Block.

DOGE
